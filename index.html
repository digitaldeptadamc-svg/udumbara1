Udumbara
Botanical Name :

  • Ficus racemosa Linn

    • Family Name :

  • Moraceae

    • Local Name :

  • Attimara

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi –Gular

  • English – Cluster Fig

    • Synonyms :

  • Redwood Fig

  • Indian Fig


    • Habitat :

  • Udumbara is commonly seen throughout India


    • External Morphology :

  • Leaves : Dark green, 7.5-15cm long, 3-6cm wide

  • Stem: Often grows straight


    • Useful Parts :

  • Twak(Bark)


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa –Kasaya

  • Guna – Guru Ruksa

  • Vipaka - Katu

  • Virya - Sita


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Astringent

  • Anthelmintic

  • Antidiuretic


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Trisna: In case of thirst originated by pittadosa, Juice of ripen fruit of Udumbara is mixed with sugar and taken internally

  • Raktapradra: Juice of Udumbara is mixed with honey is taken in case of Raktapradra


    • Dose

  • Kwatha: 50-100 ml

  • Curna: 3-6g


    • Formulation:

  • NyagrodhadiKwatha

  • NyagrodhadiCurna

  • PancavalkalaLepa
